NY-based VP of Operations at Keepe (Techstars, Series A, Prop-Tech Marketplace). Here’s a few notes of interest…

- **Operating**. I’m interested in “how the sausage is made” and sometimes write down my thoughts [here](notion:eeaba22174a246f4b709261dfb93f140).
- **Good Lighting**. This is underrated, in general.
- **Tinkering and Building**. Much of what I want to read sits online (Substacks, RSS feeds, Research Papers, etc), however, print is a much more productive form factor for me when the intention is to dive into the words on the page (and nothing else). Thus, I’ve been building [PrintStack](https://www.printstack.so/) in the wee hours and weekends. If you want a test press, [let me know](mailto:anp042@gmail.com) and I’ll send you one!
- **Tinkering some more**. I was surprised when I found out that I couldn’t sort all active listings in the US by projected monthly cash flow (Rental Income - Monthly Payments) so I began [building with Replit](https://www.loom.com/share/a40f31d3ef904b95a0caf3e2b31222bc?sid=5ce3be06-b82f-4b80-840a-39e04c0901c8).
- **Reading**. I read a fair amount, but wish I could read more. I’ve semi-recently become a member of [Goodreads](https://goodreads.com/armand-pizzicarola) (is there nothing better out there?) and sometimes add a few notes on what I read [here](notion:a7e43b46bee74e54850a8a1fdcfd192b).
- **StartupChile Founder.** A friend and I once tried our hands at a hardware startup that we took through StartupChile in 2015. Although the experience was formative (and semi-successful), I vowed never to touch hardware again (it was too dang hard!). Time will tell if that vow lasts :)
- **Geekwire Writer**. As an excuse to travel S America after graduating Seattle U, a friend and I convinced Geekwire to allow us to [publish some articles](https://www.geekwire.com/author/armand-pizzicarola-and-andrew-mewborn/) on the innovation scene down there.
- **Music**. Back in 2014, a friend and I had the opportunity to pull together a community of music lovers around Slack with [#music](https://launchpass.com/hashtagmusic), recognized on [Buzzfeed](https://www.buzzfeed.com/veronica0811/10-fast-growing-slack-communitieshangouts-you-sho-nvja) and [TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/slack-is-quietly-unintentionally-killing-irc). Nowadays, I cherish human-powered radio (s/o to [NTS](https://www.nts.live/))
