# [Reading] Free: Coming of Age at the End of History, Lea Ypi

By Armand Pizzicarola · 2023-06-27

I’ve lately (and unintentionally) found myself reading books that take place around/in the communist revolution. I have no comment on that fact, but it’s made conversations with my in-laws, who left the USSR shortly after its Dissolution a lot more interesting.

This book is good in my opinion, mainly, because it’s unclear of a particular point of view at first. I become ultimately skeptical, especially in non-fiction, when the narrative is too one-sided (even if I don’t agree with it) so for Lea to simply reflect on her experience was refreshing. In fact, it took some digging for me to figure out her complete take on communism. I haven’t read Marx, and am not *for* communism myself, but as any dutiful student of the world, it’s important to me that my exposure to different ideas is high.

In all, if you’re looking for a delicate window into how someone who has apparently struggled through communism can turn out to be *for* the idea, this is a good place to start.
