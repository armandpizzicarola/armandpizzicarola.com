[Reading] The 48 Laws of Power, Robert Greene

Armand Pizzicarola · Apr 1, 2024

Yikes…

Not sure this is ideal model for how to live your life, but as with most things, it doesn’t hurt to understand them. I wonder the weights of each of these laws over time; what has been the most effective in this decade vs. 1,000 years ago? Do some become stale overtime?

I’m sure there are countless book reports on the individual laws and how they’ve played out over time, so I won’t fancy that here, but did you know Robert Greene has a book with 50 Cent?
