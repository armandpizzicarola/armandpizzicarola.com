# The Good News is That There’s Bad News

By Armand Pizzicarola · 2023-09-28

Problems are the best thing to ever happen to us.

Sometimes, in order to settle the initial nerves, I have to remind myself and others around me, that we’re likely not the only ones facing any given problem. After that, we begin an approach.

In business, when we get a chance to solve a problem, we’re getting a chance to create value. If we execute on that chance, we have an opportunity to extract some of that value and that should be exciting.

Without problems, or in other words “bad news”, what would we do all day?
